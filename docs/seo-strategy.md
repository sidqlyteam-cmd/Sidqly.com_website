# Sidqly SEO Keyword & Page Architecture Strategy

This document details the search demand, terminology splits, query clustering, keyword-to-page mappings, content strategy frameworks, internal linking hierarchies, and quarterly update workflows for the Sidqly platform.

---

## 1. Country-Specific Search Demand Research

Global search behavior across Islamic charitable operations is highly localized, dictated by distinct terminologies, seasonal cycles, and regional tax policies.

### Pakistan
- **Key Terminology:** Primarily uses **Masjid** over Mosque, **Qurbani** over Udhiya, and **Rations / Packs** over food hampers/meal distributions. **Zakat** queries are highly active.
- **Seasonality Spikes:** Zakat searches explode during Ramadan (Pakistanis traditionally calculate and disburse Zakat during the holy month). Qurbani queries peak during Dhul Hijjah.
- **Search Intent:** Highly transactional. Focus is on operational efficiency (volunteer logistics, manual bank transfer matching, routing).

### United Kingdom (UK)
- **Key Terminology:** Balanced split between **Mosque** and **Masjid**. Uses both **Sadaqah** and **charity donation tracking**. High focus on Gift Aid logs and accounting compliance.
- **Seasonality Spikes:** Ramadan, Eid al-Adha, and the UK Tax-Year End (late March / early April), which frequently overlaps with Ramadan giving cycles.
- **Search Intent:** Highly transactional and informational. High priority placed on board-ready auditing, trustee accountability, and legal transparency.

### United States (US)
- **Key Terminology:** Balanced split between **Mosque** and **Masjid**. High volume for **Islamic charity software**, **Zakat platforms**, and **Udhiya tracking**. 501(c)(3) tax receipt records are mandatory.
- **Seasonality Spikes:** Ramadan, Eid, and US Year-End Tax Season (late December).
- **Search Intent:** Highly navigational and transactional. Focuses on advanced analytics, platform security, and seamless donor-safe update portals.

### Canada
- **Key Terminology:** Similar to US. Dominant use of **Mosque software** and **Zakat systems**. Demands official Canadian Revenue Agency (CRA) tax-compliant receipts.
- **Seasonality Spikes:** Ramadan and late December (Tax Year End).
- **Search Intent:** Informational. Highly interested in privacy, donor data ownership, and row-restricted field maps.

### Australia
- **Key Terminology:** Balanced split between Mosque/Masjid. High frequency for **volunteer coordination** and **Ramadan food drive** trackers.
- **Seasonality Spikes:** Ramadan and late June (Australian Financial Year End).
- **Search Intent:** Transactional. Focused on mobile usability, quick field uploader speeds, and secure receipting.

### Gulf Region (MENA / GCC)
- **Key Terminology:** Dominant use of **Masjid**, **Zakat management**, and **Udhiya** (over Qurbani). High preference for custom board reporting and corporate-sponsor integrations.
- **Seasonality Spikes:** Ramadan and Dhul Hijjah.
- **Search Intent:** Navigational. High emphasis on corporate CSR portals, Shariah scholar compliance logs, and bulk vendor tasking.

## 2. Query Clustering & Intent Classification

We organize our search universe into 9 major intent-based clusters:

1. **Islamic Charity Software**
   - *Primary:* `Islamic charity management software`
   - *Secondary:* `Muslim NGO software`, `halal fundraising tools`
   - *Intent:* Transactional
   - *Audience:* Executive Directors, Trustees, Tech Officers

2. **Mosque/Masjid Management Software**
   - *Primary:* `mosque management software`
   - *Secondary:* `masjid donation platform`, `mosque membership tool`
   - *Intent:* Transactional
   - *Audience:* Mosque Committee Members, Treasurers, Imams

3. **Zakat Systems**
   - *Primary:* `Zakat separation system`
   - *Secondary:* `Zakat calculation software`, `Tamleek verification tracker`
   - *Intent:* Informational / Transactional
   - *Audience:* Finance Officers, Scholars, Zakat Accountants

4. **Qurbani Management**
   - *Primary:* `Qurbani management software`
   - *Secondary:* `Udhiya portion tracker`, `slaughter certificate generator`
   - *Intent:* Transactional
   - *Audience:* Qurbani Campaign Directors, Livestock Sourcing Leads

5. **Ramadan Distribution**
   - *Primary:* `Ramadan donation management`
   - *Secondary:* `family ration pack tracker`, `food box delivery router`
   - *Intent:* Transactional
   - *Audience:* Operations Managers, Volunteer Coordinators

6. **Donation Verification**
   - *Primary:* `donation verification software`
   - *Secondary:* `screenshot reconciliation ledger`, `bank transfer check`
   - *Intent:* Informational
   - *Audience:* Treasurers, Financial Auditors, Bookkeepers

7. **Beneficiary Management & Recipient Privacy**
   - *Primary:* `recipient privacy software`
   - *Secondary:* `beneficiary face blurring`, `EXIF data scrubber`
   - *Intent:* Informational
   - *Audience:* Modesty Compliance Managers, Data Protection Officers

8. **Proof of Delivery**
   - *Primary:* `charity proof of delivery tracker`
   - *Secondary:* `field photo uploader`, `donor evidence validation`
   - *Intent:* Transactional
   - *Audience:* Campaign Field Directors, Auditing leads

9. **Impact Reporting**
   - *Primary:* `charity board pack generator`
   - *Secondary:* `nonprofit impact report`, `sponsor trust reporting`
   - *Intent:* Informational
   - *Audience:* Communications Directors, Corporate CSR Sponsors, Board Chairs

## 5. Internal Linking Plan

To establish high topical authority and funnel visitors seamlessly:

1. **Features & Utilities ──>** `/guided-pilot`
   - Features page highlights modular tracking and links directly to the `/guided-pilot` sign-up form.
   - Calculators (e.g. Zakat Calculator) link to the `/modules/zakat-fund-separation` landing page as educational references.
2. **Blog & Educational Guides ──>** `/modules/*`
   - Blog posts (such as Ramadan planning guides) contain internal context links to `/modules/ramadan-meals-rations` and `/modules/proof-trust-engine`.
   - General resources (e.g. Eid Giving playbooks) link back to `/modules/qurbani-lifecycle` for full operational solutions.
3. **Core Solutions ──>** `/guided-pilot` or `/contact-sales`
   - Mid-tier plans map directly to the `/guided-pilot` workflow.
   - Enterprise listings map to the `/contact-sales` page for personalized onboarding setups.

## 6. Quarterly Refresh Workflow

We execute a structured four-step review at the start of each quarter:

### Step 1: Pull Search Console Metrics
Export Search Console queries filtering for impressions, click counts, average positions, and Click-Through Rates (CTR). Group queries into their pre-defined 9 clusters.

### Step 2: Compare Terminology & Google Trends
Monitor Google Trends to detect rising localized term variances (e.g., shifts in UK search volumes from "Masjid software" to "Mosque software"). Adjust metadata dynamically to capture new terms without altering layout.

### Step 3: Audit Keyword Cannibalization
Run checks to verify that no two public pages are ranking for the same primary focus keywords. Resolve duplicates by consolidation or strict internal linking adjustments.

### Step 4: Address Conversion Gaps
Cross-reference GTM click events and `trackEvent` records with organic traffic. If a primary page receives high impressions but low conversion clicks, optimize CTA readability, visible labels, or load times.
